courtesy translation.)

> **Legal details:** [Full legal name — commercial registration — registered address — to be completed before publication]
> **Data protection contact:** `privacy@mahwar.app` · `support@mahwar.app`

At Mahwar we collect the minimum data needed to operate a roadside-assistance service. We do not
sell it and do not use it for advertising or tracking. This Policy explains what we collect, why,
how we store it, and your rights of control. Read it together with our **Terms of Use**, which form
an integral part of your agreement with us.

---

## 1) Scope

1.1. This Policy applies to the Mahwar mobile apps (Android and iOS), the `mahwar.app` website and
any related service.

1.2. It does not apply to third-party sites or apps you may reach through links in the app; each has
its own policy.

1.3. The Platform targets persons aged at least **eighteen (18)** within the **Arab Republic of
Egypt**. We do not knowingly collect data from minors; anyone who discovers that a minor created an
account should notify us so we can delete it.

---

## 2) Data We Collect

### 2.1. Data you provide

| Data | When collected | Mandatory? |
|---|---|---|
| **Egyptian phone number** | At registration and OTP sign-in | Mandatory — no account without it |
| **Full name** | When completing your profile | Optional, but shown to the other party in a Request |
| **Email address** | Optional in the profile or for account recovery | Optional |
| **National ID** | Service Providers, and Car Owners before their first Request (identity verification) | Mandatory before requesting |
| **Vehicle data** | Make, model, year, category, colour, plate letters and digits | Mandatory for Car Owners |
| **Vehicle location, coordinates and text address** | When creating a Request | Mandatory for a Request |
| **Fault description, notes and photos** | When creating a Request | Description mandatory; photos optional |
| **Support ticket image, subject and text** | When contacting support | Optional |
| **Ratings and comments** | After completing a Request | Optional |

The OTP sent to your number only verifies that the number is yours. We do not read your messages or
call logs and do not request access to them.

### 2.2. Data collected automatically

- **Request data:** status, offered and accepted price, creation, acceptance, completion and
  cancellation timestamps.
- **Location during a Request:** vehicle location and Service Provider location only while an active
  Request is being performed. No background location and no location outside the Request.
- **Notification data:** Firebase Cloud Messaging token, device identifier, platform, last-seen time
  and the text of notifications sent to you.
- **Internal usage data:** screen name opened, entry time and dwell time — to improve the app and
  detect faults, with no advertising tracking or marketing cookies.
- **SMS send logs:** number, message type, delivery result and time — for operations, send quotas and
  abuse prevention.
- **Basic technical data:** IP address and connection data that our servers and our infrastructure
  providers record automatically for security and fraud prevention.

### 2.3. Data we never collect

We never collect or request: payment-card data (there are no in-app payments), contacts, SMS
messages, call logs, audio or video, health or fitness data, financial data, background location or
the advertising identifier. We do not sell your data or share it with ad networks or data brokers.

## 4) Who Sees Your Data

### 4.1. The other party to a Request

- **A Service Provider** sees the fault description, vehicle location and photos, the Car Owner's
  name and the price. **The Car Owner's phone number is revealed only after the Request is
  confirmed**, solely to perform it; the provider may not store it or use it after the Request ends
  or for marketing.
- **A Car Owner** sees the Service Provider's name, public rating and offer details, and contact
  details within the Request to the extent the app provides them.

### 4.2. Public content

Your ratings and comments on a Service Provider are **public** on that provider's profile, tied to
your in-app display name, and may count toward a rating average over a defined time window. Public
content never displays phone numbers, national IDs, vehicle data or locations.

### 4.3. Technology processors (acting on our behalf)

| Processor | What it processes | Location |
|---|---|---|
| **Supabase** | Database, authentication, storage, cloud functions | Cloud data centres (outside Egypt) |
| **Google Firebase (FCM)** | Mobile push delivery | Google — outside Egypt |
| **ZADX** | Verification and alert SMS | Local provider |
| **Resend** | Delivering support-ticket email to the company inbox | Outside Egypt |
| **Cloudflare** | DNS hosting and email routing | Outside Egypt |
| **OpenStreetMap** | Map tiles | Sees the IP of the device requesting a tile; does not see your account data |

These processors handle data per our instructions and only for operating the service. We do not sell
your data or share it for their marketing.

### 4.4. Legal disclosure

We may disclose data under a binding legal request (court order or competent regulator) or to protect
our rights and users' safety, to the minimum extent necessary. We review every request and refuse
unlawful ones.

### 4.5. Business transfer

If the company merges, is acquired, or its assets are sold, data may transfer to the successor for
the same purpose and under the same obligations stated here, with notice before the transfer.

## 5) Where Your Data Is Stored

5.1. Your data is stored on Supabase cloud infrastructure and may be outside Egypt. We rely on
appropriate contractual and technical safeguards for transfers.

5.2. Data in transit is protected by encryption (HTTPS/TLS), and database data is protected by
row-level security (RLS) policies that prevent any user from reading another user's data. Administrative
access is limited to authorised staff, recorded in an audit log and protected by multi-factor
authentication.

## 6) Retention

| Data | Retention |
|---|---|
| Account data (profile, vehicle, notifications, device token) | For the life of the account; deleted when it is closed |
| Requests, offers and ratings | For the life of the account; retained **anonymised** after deletion where needed for service records and fraud prevention |
| Public comments on Service Providers | Remain after deletion in **anonymised** form, as they are part of the provider's public rating |
| Support tickets | Remain after deletion in **anonymised** form for dispute reference |
| SMS send logs | Remain **anonymised** for abuse prevention and quota enforcement |
| Request and support photos/attachments | Deleted when the account is deleted or the purpose ends |
| Usage data (screens) | A limited operational period |
| Security and audit logs | As required by law and for a reasonable period |

We keep data for as long as needed for the purpose, a legal obligation or defending claims, then
delete it or anonymise it so it cannot be linked to you.

## 7) Deleting Your Account and Data

7.1. You can delete your account at any time from **the in-app settings**, or via
`mahwar.app/delete-account` without installing the app.

7.2. To confirm you own the account we ask for a code sent to your **registered phone number** (the
same sign-in mechanism). We do not ask for a password because the account may have been created with
a phone number only.

7.3. On deletion we remove: profile and vehicle data, your Requests and their details, your
notifications and device tokens, your preferences, your security question if any, and your stored
attachments. We also permanently remove your account from our authentication systems.

7.4. **Limited anonymised information** remains after deletion — as explained in Section 6 — for
service records, abuse prevention, legal obligations and defending claims, and cannot be linked to
you once your identifiers are removed.

7.5. We may temporarily refuse deletion if an active Request must be completed, a dispute exists or a
legal obligation applies; we will explain the reason and the delay.

7.6. Deletion is usually permanent and cannot be undone. Create a new account if you wish to return.

## 8) Your Rights

Under Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law No. 151 of 2020 and applicable regulations, you have the
right to:

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you.
- **Rectify** inaccurate or incomplete data.
- **Delete** your data and close your account (Section 7).
- **Restrict or object to** processing for reasons specific to you, subject to our legal obligations.
- **Withdraw consent** where processing is based on consent, without affecting the lawfulness of
  prior processing.
- **Port** data you provided in a readable format, where applicable.
- **Complain** to us first at `privacy@mahwar.app`, then to the competent authority (the National
  Data Protection Center) if your complaint is unresolved.

8.7. We usually respond to requests within **30 days**, extendable for complexity with the reason
communicated. We may ask for proof of identity before acting to protect your data.

8.8. We do not treat users differently for exercising their rights.

## 10) Security

10.1. We use encryption in transit, strict access policies, least-privilege administrative access,
multi-factor authentication for administrator accounts, and audit logs for sensitive actions.

10.2. No one can guarantee absolute security. If a breach affects your data, we notify you and the
competent authority as required by law.

10.3. You are responsible for protecting your device and verification code; never share the OTP with
anyone — Mahwar never asks for it outside the verification screen.

## 11) Notifications

11.1. We send operational notifications about Requests and your account (Request updates,
verification messages). You can adjust notification permissions in your device settings.

11.2. We may send alert SMS when a Request needs your action, with daily caps to avoid nuisance.

11.3. We do not use your data to send third-party advertising.

## 12) Your Location and the Map

12.1. We use vehicle location to find nearby providers and track the Request. We do not follow your
location outside an active Request, in the background, or after the Request is cancelled or completed.

12.2. The map is displayed using OpenStreetMap tiles; the map service may see the connection address
needed to fetch the tile, but not your account data.
